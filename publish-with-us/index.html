Questions

Before you apply.

Still unsure? Read the full sponsor policy or open the media kit.

Is sponsored content labeled?

Yes. Every paid story is clearly labeled as sponsored or partner content. Labeling is not optional.

Do you guarantee a link, ranking or placement?

No. We do not sell guaranteed rankings, dofollow links or editorial awards. Link treatment is confirmed during review.

When do I pay?

Only after the editorial team accepts your angle and evidence. Payment is requested through PayPal at that point, not before.

Can the company edit the article?

No. Partners supply facts and interview material. Decisions on framing, evidence and wording stay with Interface Report.
