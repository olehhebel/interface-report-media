Editorial Guidelines

The operating rules behind how we report, analyze, review and update our work.

Last reviewed September 11, 2026Interface Report editorial desk
Transparent standards
Visible by design

Priority order

Accuracy comes first. Original value, reader usefulness, topical relevance, clarity, timeliness, search discoverability and commercial value follow in that order.

Sources and evidence

We prefer primary sources: official documentation, filings, research papers, standards, direct interviews, product documentation and first-party data. Secondary sources are used to add context, not replace the strongest available evidence.

AI-assisted work

AI may assist research organization, transcription, summarization, drafting support, editing and production. It must not invent quotes, tests, statistics, product use, sources or people. Material factual claims still require verification.

Reviews and hands-on claims

We do not imply hands-on testing unless hands-on testing actually happened. Reviews disclose relevant methodology, limitations and commercial relationships.

Commercial independence

Sponsors may pay for clearly labeled commercial formats. They do not buy our editorial opinion, rankings or undisclosed favorable treatment.
