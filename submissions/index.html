Submission Guidelines

A practical route for experts, founders and teams to pitch useful material without turning the publication into a guest-post farm.

Last reviewed September 11, 2026Interface Report editorial desk
Transparent standards
Visible by design

What to pitch

Pitch a specific idea with a reader problem, a clear thesis and evidence you can uniquely contribute. Original data, documented product lessons, credible technical breakdowns and named expert perspectives are stronger than generic thought leadership.

What to include

Send a working headline, 3–5 sentence summary, why you are qualified to write it, any original data or primary sources, and whether a commercial relationship exists.

What we reject

Mass-produced SEO articles, undisclosed paid links, generic AI-written listicles, republished vendor copy, fabricated case studies and articles whose primary purpose is link placement.
