Privacy Policy

How Interface Report handles newsletter, commercial inquiry, analytics and site-use data.

Last reviewed September 12, 2026Interface Report editorial desk
Minimize collection
Use data for stated purpose
No invented consent

Commercial inquiries

When you submit an advertising or sponsored-content application, we may collect the company or product name, product URL, campaign goal or proposed angle, contact email, optional Telegram username, selected package, attribution data and an application identifier. We use this information to review fit, respond to the inquiry, operate the commercial workflow and keep a record of the submission.

Telegram routing

Commercial inquiries may be forwarded to the Interface Report commercial desk in Telegram so the editorial team can review and respond. If you choose to continue through Telegram, Telegram processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

Newsletter

When newsletter subscriptions are activated, email addresses and basic signup attribution may be sent to our newsletter service provider, currently planned as Beehiiv, for subscription management, confirmation, delivery and unsubscribe handling. The subscription flow uses email confirmation. If the provider changes, this policy should be updated before the new provider processes subscriber data.

Analytics

The site contains hooks for measuring newsletter and advertiser-funnel events, but analytics should only be treated as active when a production analytics container is actually configured. We aim to minimize unnecessary tracking and will add consent controls where required before enabling non-essential or personalized tracking.

Hosting and technical logs

Hosting and serverless infrastructure may process standard request information needed to deliver the website, prevent abuse, diagnose errors and operate submitted forms. Do not send sensitive personal or payment information through editorial or advertising brief fields.

Payments

Interface Report does not collect card details in its application forms. If a commercial application is accepted, payment may be completed through PayPal. Payment information is then processed by PayPal under its own terms and privacy practices.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell submitted contact information as a data product. Information may be shared with service providers used to operate the site, newsletter, communications, analytics or payments only as needed for those functions.

Your choices

Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe using the link provided in newsletter messages. For reasonable questions about a commercial submission or privacy request, use the publication Contact page.

Changes

This policy should be revised when material providers, tracking practices or data flows change. The review date above indicates the current published version.
