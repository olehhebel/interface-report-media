Advertising Policy

Advertising can fund the publication. It cannot buy our editorial judgment, hidden links or invented audience claims.

What we sell

Interface Report may sell clearly identified sponsor placements, sponsored editorial products, selected distribution, newsletter sponsorship and other audience-access products that fit the publication.

Review before payment

Commercial submissions are reviewed for topical fit, evidence quality, legal and reputation risk before payment is requested. Accepted direct campaigns currently use PayPal as the preferred payment method. Payment does not guarantee a positive conclusion, ranking influence or publication outside the agreed scope.

Disclosure

Paid or materially sponsored editorial is labeled clearly near the content it qualifies. Preview cards and newsletter promotions preserve the commercial disclosure where needed.

Links

Paid outbound links use rel="sponsored" by default. We do not sell dofollow guarantees, PageRank influence, hidden backlinks or search-ranking promises.

Inventory and user experience

Advertising must not overwhelm editorial content, create deceptive interface patterns, trigger forced redirects, autoplay audio or materially damage page performance. Native sponsor units must remain visually distinguishable from independent reporting.

Audience claims and reporting

Media Kit numbers and campaign reports use verified data only. Early-stage status is stated plainly when reliable audience data is not yet available.

Restricted categories

We reject deceptive, illegal, malicious or clearly harmful promotions, including scams, malware, gambling, adult services, misleading medical claims, questionable financial schemes, obvious link spam and irrelevant material that would damage reader trust.

Refunds and cancellation

If a paid campaign cannot proceed under the agreed scope, the commercial desk will resolve the order through revision, rescheduling or refund as appropriate. We do not use payment to force publication of material that fails policy review.

For sponsored editorial-specific standards, see the Sponsored Content Policy. For current packages, see Advertise.
