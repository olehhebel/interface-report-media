Corrections Policy

How we handle factual errors, clarifications and material updates.

Last reviewed September 11, 2026Interface Report editorial desk
Transparent standards
Visible by design

Report a correction

Send the URL, the statement you believe is incorrect, the reason, and any primary evidence available. We review material corrections as an editorial priority.

What changes look like

Minor copy edits may be made silently when they do not alter meaning. Factual corrections or meaningful clarifications are disclosed on the article when the change affects the reader’s understanding.

Updated dates

We change an article’s updated date only for meaningful editorial changes, not cosmetic edits designed to simulate freshness.
