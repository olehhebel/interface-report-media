What we cover

Interface Report covers AI products, product design and UX, AI agents, Human–AI interaction, engineering, startups, SaaS and the business decisions around technology.

We prioritize material that helps readers understand how products work, where they fail, what design choices matter and what a technical or business shift means in practice.

What we are not

We are not a guest-post directory, backlink marketplace or pay-to-rank review site. Commercial content is a minority of the publication and is labeled wherever payment or material sponsorship is involved.

Who we write for

Our core readers are founders, product leaders, designers, engineers and operators working on AI-native and software products.

How we build trust

Every article has a named author, publication date, topic, reading time and a visible correction path. Material claims should be traceable to primary or authoritative sources whenever those sources exist.
