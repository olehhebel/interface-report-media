Editorial controls

Payment does not guarantee publication. Every paid story is screened for relevance, evidence, reputation risk and claim quality. Paid links are qualified with rel="sponsored" by default. We do not sell ranking influence, hidden advertorials or guaranteed positive coverage.

What we do not publish

We reject obvious link spam, gambling, adult content, malware, scams, deceptive financial schemes, illegal businesses, false medical claims, irrelevant industries and content that would damage reader trust.

Launch distribution

Current owned channels are the publication website and RSS, with newsletter infrastructure being connected. Homepage, newsletter and social placements are offered only when those channels are actually active and available; we do not sell hypothetical reach.
