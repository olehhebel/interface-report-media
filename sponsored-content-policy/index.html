Clear labels

Paid or materially sponsored content is labeled Sponsored, Partner Content or another equally explicit disclosure at the top of the page.

Links and search integrity

Paid outbound links use appropriate sponsored link attributes. We do not sell dofollow guarantees, hidden backlinks or paid placement disguised as independent editorial.

Editorial control

We may reject, edit, fact-check or request substantiation for commercial claims. Payment does not guarantee a positive conclusion or placement in independent editorial rankings.

Restricted categories

We do not accept deceptive, illegal, malicious or obviously harmful promotional material, and we avoid categories that would undermine reader trust or publication safety.
